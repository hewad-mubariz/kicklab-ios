Adjust

Spotlight Ball

Pull attention toward the ball with color isolation and a soft focus falloff.

Adjust
Focus size · Background color · Feather
Needs
Stable ball mask; the existing player mask is optional.
04

Heat Wake

A short refractive air wake bends the existing background behind the moving ball.

Adjust
Distortion · Wake length · Falloff
Needs
Smooth tracked direction and ball scale, plus a background pixel-warp renderer.
05

Orbit Rings

Decorative light rings wrap around the tracked ball with convincing foreground and background layering.

Adjust
Ring size · Orbit motion · Color
Needs
Precise ball silhouette and screen-space scale; decorative motion does not measure spin.
06

Turf Burst

A tightly timed burst of grass or dust gives the strike a physical punch.

Adjust
Strike frame · Burst strength · Turf or dust
Needs
A confirmed strike and local ground alignment, with foreground occlusion.
07

Net Pulse

A confirmed net hit sends a controlled light ripple through the goal mesh.

Adjust
Impact frame · Ripple size · Duration
Needs
A confirmed net impact and goal/net alignment in addition to ball tracking.
08

Energy Gate

Place an artistic light gate at an observed point in the ball’s flight.

Adjust
Gate position · Gate size · Color
Needs
Ball track and masks; stable or aligned footage helps place the gate.
09

Ghost Duel

Compare two aligned recordings through synchronized photographic ball ghosts.

Adjust
Reference clip · Contact sync · Ghost opacity
Needs
Two recordings with a matching camera view or reliable alignment and tracked balls.
10

Hero Sequence

An editable highlight preset assembles run-up, strike, flight and finish into a short story.

Adjust
Story length · Pacing · Effect strength
Needs
Confirmed event timing and reliable tracking; combines editable effects into chapters.

Future creative concepts. The illustrated ball, footage and controls are generated design references, not completed app features or verified measurements.
