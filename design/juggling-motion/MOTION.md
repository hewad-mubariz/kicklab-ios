# Juggle Dude — Juggling flow motion

Scope: capture → processing → replay editor → tool sheets → Save & Share. Presentation only; recording, tracking, playback and export behavior are unchanged. Pre-change copies of every touched file are in `original/`.

## Tokens (kicklab/Record/SessionMotion.swift)

| Token | Curve | Used for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `snap` | snappy 0.28 s, +0.12 bounce | control morphs, symbol swaps, selection |
| `pop` | spring 0.36 s, damping 0.7 | panels, stickers, toolbox, placement glides |
| `settle` | spring 0.44 s, damping 0.86 | large surfaces (preview blur) |
| `fade` | ease-out 0.18 s | every animation under Reduce Motion |
| `tray` | spring 0.4 s, damping 0.86 | Customize tray height and the video above it |
| `stagger` | 45 ms per sibling | entrances |

Helpers: `.sessionEntrance(shown, order:)`, the `.sessionPop` / `.sessionRise` / `.sessionDrop` / `.sessionTuck` transitions, `.sessionKick(trigger)`, `SessionPressStyle` (fast dip, springy release), and `.exportZoomSource(ns)`.

Rules: things move only when they arrive or when their state changes. They do not keep moving afterwards. Exceptions that show work in progress: the REC dot pulses while recording, and spinners turn while busy. Reduce Motion keeps every opacity change and drops every offset, scale, blur, rotation and kick. Home keeps `HomePressStyle` (160 ms); the session flow uses `SessionPressStyle`.

## Choreography

- **Capture HUD**: top bar drops in, metrics rise, controls rise last.
- **Record**: the red mark twists −90° while squeezing from circle to stop-square. One red ring bursts outward. Gallery and Flip tuck in toward the shutter and spring back out on Stop. The record button stays fixed in the center. Heavy haptic on Record and Stop, selection haptic when the button returns to ready.
- **Session badge**: "REC ·" slides in and the glass capsule grows to fit it. The dot turns red and pulses.
- **Counter**: digits roll. Each touch kicks the number (+8 %, leading anchor) and floats a +1 up and away.
- **Processing** (kicklab/Record/ProcessingOverlay.swift, "Ball Path", picked from design/processing-concepts): the background appears instantly (no black gap after Stop). The header shows the percentage rolling with numeric transitions, and the current step blur-swaps beneath it. Below it, a path of S-curves runs from a kick-off spot through four checkpoints: Saving your video, Tracking the ball, Counting touches, Building your replay. Entrance: the dotted track unrolls in 0.95 s and each checkpoint pops in as the track reaches it; once its 3D image is ready, the ball drops from above onto the kick-off spot and settles. While a step runs, the ball creeps toward its checkpoint, slowing as it nears, so the wait always looks alive. When the step really finishes, it rolls the rest of the way at a steady pace; it never moves backward. The road behind is lit (deep green to mint, with glow and a white comet head); the dots ahead drift forward. The ball rolls true to distance and sheds sparkles. The active checkpoint pings like sonar under a turning arc. On arrival, the node pops and fills mint, a shockwave and 12 sparks fire, the ball hops, the check draws itself in, the status blurs from "In progress" to "Done", a light haptic plays, and the ball rolls clear so the check stays visible. The last checkpoint bursts gold with two rings and 26 sparks, the header turns to "Ready!" with a success haptic, and the screen lifts away 0.36 s later; RecordView hands over to the editor 0.8 s after completion. At the bottom, a Coach's tip blur-swaps every 5 s with a thin countdown bar. Reduce Motion keeps the progress and checks without unrolling, drops, sparks or hops.
- **Hand-off**: the editor cover appears without the modal slide. The video settles from 1.04× and the chrome builds in. On "Record another session" the camera HUD builds back in.
- **Editor**: Customize and the toolbox share one glass ID inside a `GlassEffectContainer`, so the pill morphs into the panel and back. Tiles land in order. Selecting the counter snaps the handles inward from 1.3×. Play/pause, undo/check and the Customize label morph in place. Reset and the position presets glide the sticker with `pop`. Removing or restoring the counter pops it at its own center.
- **Customize panel**: Ball, Effects, Timer and Graph open in place, with no sheet, and the panel behaves like a tray. Its height is measured from the page being shown and glides to fit on one spring (`SessionMotion.tray`, 0.4 s, damping 0.86); the outgoing page never sizes it. The old page blurs (10 pt), shrinks to 0.97 and fades in 0.14 s; the new one settles in sharp over 0.3 s, starting 50 ms later, leaning 14 pt in the direction of travel (into a tool or back out). The title swaps the same way. While the panel is open the video shrinks into the space above it on the same tray spring, so the two move as one. Ball, Effects and Counter apply picks live. Counter is a page too: all twelve styles in a four-column grid with a Show switch, while the clip loops so each style's touch move plays on real touches. Placement stays on the video: tap the counter to drag, pinch, twist or remove it. When the counter is selected, its "Counter style" pill morphs into the tray, opened straight on the Counter page. The old sheet's size, rotation and position sliders are gone; the sheet still used by Save & Share and the environment preview keeps its gesture canvas and the same inline style grid.
- **Effects page**: all 11 effects (None plus 10) in the same four-column grid as the balls, three rows, with the intensity slider below. Four columns keep the panel short enough that the looping clip stays large, since the point is seeing the effect on the moving ball.
- **Ball page**: all 11 balls in a four-column grid with roomy rows, so they can be compared without scrolling. The clip holds a mid-juggle frame here rather than looping, because drawing a replacement ball onto every video frame is CPU work on the main thread. The pick shows at once (label, pad, flight); the video takes the new ball 0.8 s later, after the pick moment has played.
- **Ball pick** (kicklab/Record/BallPickSlot.swift): the tap is a rigid kick haptic. The ball swings round a wide "around the world" loop in 0.42 s: out about one ball-width, up about 1.2 ball-heights, then over the top and back to its spot. The left two columns swing right first and the right two swing left, so no ball leaves the panel. It leans up to 28° into the swing and comes toward you as it rises, up to 1.5× at the top; it is never drawn smaller than at rest or squashed. It spins with juggling backspin: 2½ turns end over end in 0.48 s, front panels rolling up toward you, around an axis tilted with the swing for a touch of sidespin. The spin is fastest off the kick and eases into the landing, rendered from the ball's real 3D orientation and runs cold while it flies: an ice glow grows around it. An ice trail follows the actual path, built from a soft blue glow, an ice body, a white-hot core, and frost sparkles. Its shadow shrinks and softens with height. On touchdown: a cold flash behind the ball, two frost ripples spreading flat across the floor in perspective, ice shards thrown left and right off the rim, a small hop and pulse, and a medium thud haptic. The ball then stands on a softly lit ice pad that replaces the old selection ring. Every other ball rests on its own soft shadow. The selected label stays mint. Spin frames use a 144 px render of the same material and are re-drawn sharp at rest. Returning to the page never replays a pick. Reduce Motion keeps only the lit pad.
- **Effect icons**: each effect is a football with the effect streaming behind it. The shipping Metal shaders draw it (kicklab/Effects/EffectIconRenderer.swift) using the ball you picked, with feathered edges. The old stadium photo cards are gone.
- **Download** (kicklab/Record/ReplayDownloader.swift, direction A in design/export-concepts): the lime download button in the editor saves at once, with no Save & Share page and no Original/Edited choice. It renders exactly what the editor shows (effects, ball, environment and counter) at 1080p and adds it to Photos. The button morphs into a glass pill with a progress ring and a rolling percentage, then into a mint check, with a medium haptic on start and a success haptic when saved. A "Saved to Photos" toast with a frame of the clip drops in under the top bar for 2.8 s. The share panel rises in place of the playback controls: Stories, TikTok, Messages and More (all open the iOS share sheet for now), plus Record another and Done; its X returns to editing. Tapping the check reopens the panel without saving again; any edit after saving turns the check back into the download button. Failures show a warning toast. Leaving the Effects, Counter or Ball page now returns the playhead to where it was before the page's preview moved it.
- **Sign-in** (kicklab/Account/SignInView.swift, direction B in design/signin-concepts): first launch and Profile show the same screen. From Profile, "Sign in" closes the sheet and the app swaps Home for this screen; "Try it first" reads "Not now" there and returns to Home. Entrance (after the launch halo): the wordmark drops a few points, the ball falls from above the screen and lands in 0.46 s with a squash, bounces twice (16 % then 4 % of its size) and unwinds 160° of spin; the flight curve draws itself over 1 s; the heading and the glass tray rise in, staggered. At rest the ball bobs slowly (2.8 s) and rocks ±3°, and its shadow shrinks as it rises. Every choice lives in one Liquid Glass tray over a soft lime glow. Tapping email grows the tray into the email form with the Customize tray's swap and spring; the heading blurs out, the ball steps back to about 140 pt and shrinks further as the keyboard lifts the tray. A valid address pops a check into the field and lights Send lime with an arrow; sending shows a spinner, drops the keyboard, swaps the tray to "Check your inbox" with a resend ring counting down, and the ball hops and turns once. Apple or Google: the tapped button shows "Waiting for…" with a spinner, the others dim to 35 %, and the ball spins inside two lime arcs. Signed in: the tray sinks away, the heading blur-swaps to "You're in.", three lime rings burst and the ball is kicked up and off screen; Home fades in 1.1 s later. Selection haptic per step, success haptic on sign-in. Reduce Motion keeps fades only: no drop, bob, spin, hop or kick.
- **Leaderboard** (kicklab/Leaderboard, "Club Pennants" from design/leaderboard-studies v3 #15): opens from the trophy button beside the profile button on Home with a zoom transition and shrinks back into it. The top three hang as pennants from their own rods on warm clubhouse paper (graphite in dark mode): #1 lime with laurels around the score, #2 night green with gold fringe, #3 silver. On opening they start rolled up on the rods and unfurl one after another (#2 at 0.04 s, #3 at 0.13 s, the winner last at 0.26 s) on a springy drop (0.5 s, bounce 0.3), each swinging 5° and settling like cloth (bounce 0.6). A tap pushes a pennant into a swing with a light haptic. Ranks 4 and down are list rows with a small pennant tab for the rank, cascading in. Your row is pinned at the bottom in lime with the gap to the next player ("15 to pass Ravi") and slides away while your real row is on screen. Period is one track (This week / All time) with a sliding pill and selection haptic; Global or your country is a dropdown at the top right. Changing either rolls the pennants up, swaps the board while they are hidden and hangs it again; scores roll with numeric transitions. Avatars are profile pictures: your own comes from Profile (tap the avatar, PhotosPicker, stored as a 512 px square on the device; press and hold to remove). Anyone without a picture gets initials on a colour picked from their name, or the profile mark when there is no name. Players and scores are sample data until scores are stored with accounts. Reduce Motion: no unfurl or swing.
- **Power Shot editor** (kicklab/PowerShot; trails in kicklab/Effects/Metal/ShotTrails.h): built like the juggling editor and moving the same way, with two Customize tools, Effects and Graph, and no balls, counter or timer. Entry: Home → Power Shot → Import (always available, even while recording; a running roll is stopped and saved) or a finished recording; the camera screen has no My videos any more. The upright copy of a recording is made as the processing screen's first step. The processing screen is the juggling one with shot labels ("Processing your shot": Saving your shot, Finding the ball, Tracing the flight, Building your replay, and shooting tips); it runs while every frame is tracked with the shipping ball tracker, celebrates the last checkpoint and hands over in place 1.2 s later. The track is kept beside the shot, so reopening skips processing. The editor opens paused on the end of the flight, with the whole trail in the air; the video settles from 1.04× and the chrome builds in. Customize morphs into the tray (same glass ID, tray spring, blur-and-lean page swap, staggered tiles). Effects: ten trails plus None in the four-column grid, each tile drawn by the real shader on a short flight, with the check pop, tap kick, selection haptic and the intensity slider; the clip loops from 0.6 s before the kick while this page is open. Trails run from the strike spot to the ball, thickest at the ball and tapering along their own length, and never draw on or around the ball: no ring, just the trail. Width follows the ball's size now, never thinner than 2.2 % of the frame. They show only while the ball travels faster than about 3–8 of its radii a second, bridge tracker misses of up to 0.3 s (common right after the strike), and fade over 0.45 s once the ball is lost. Effect tiles show a stadium at dusk (floodlight panels, stands, goal, mown grass) with the real trail shader sweeping from the boot up to a big ball; every tile shows its effect at once, and a pick kicks its ball across the tile again. Graph: in the spirit of the juggling motion styles, simple and graphical, each showing the whole shot at once (what is still to come drawn faintly) and lighting up with the playhead, in the chosen effect's colours, with looping thumbnails. Comet (default): a night-sky panel; the flight runs left to right at its real height in the picture as a comet, thick and hot at the ball, shedding sparks; KICK! flashes with a shockwave at the strike, and a starburst plus the time in the air pin the landing. Ball Motion: the juggling line graph for one shot, on a monitor grid: the ball's height in the picture from a second before the kick, flat while it rests, then the launch; KICK and end ticks with the time in the air between them, and a playhead. Kick Pulse: the shot as a heartbeat: speed across the picture on an ECG grid, calm before, a spike at the kick marked KICK!, decaying as the ball flies away; the head dot beats. Rainbow: the flight as a band of colour that cycles along it, sparkles of every colour at the landing. Fireworks: a rocket waits on the ground with its fuse fizzing, climbs while the ball flies and bursts when it lands, bigger the farther the ball went from the camera ("2.0× FARTHER"). Tape Measure (recorded rolls only, the default there): a yellow tape unrolls with the experimental ARKit distance, ticks every 10 cm, the ball rolling ahead; READY before the roll, gold FINAL at the end. The panel under the video shows Flight tracked, Farther (×) and Curve. No graph shows a physical speed: like the lab research, the app withholds km/h until it can be validated. All values are in the picture: no metres or km/h. The download button renders the trail at 1080p with sound and saves to Photos (progress ring, saved toast with a frame, share panel); changing the trail or its intensity offers saving again.
