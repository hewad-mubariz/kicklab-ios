Use case: ui-mockup
Asset type: one polished, high-resolution splash-screen comparison sheet for the Juggle Dude iPhone app.
Primary request: six distinct, restrained, implementable splash-screen concepts using the approved Lift football logo, with a dark and light version of EACH concept, all on ONE sheet so the user can choose. This is a static design proposal, not implementation.
Input image 1 role: exact brand-mark reference. Reuse the recognizable white abstract lifting boot, detached toe sole cut, and the smaller football floating above its toe with curved black panels and one lime seam. Preserve the boot silhouette, ball-to-boot proportions and positions faithfully. Remove the square icon backdrop when using the mark on the screens; never show it as a rounded app-icon tile. In light screens use a charcoal boot, with the white textured ball and charcoal/lime curved seams kept readable.

Composition: a landscape presentation board approximately 3072 by 2304 pixels, warm pale gray paper background, generous even margins, six numbered concept groups arranged in 3 columns and 2 rows. Each group contains TWO tall flat iPhone screen silhouettes side by side, DARK left and LIGHT right, twelve total phones. Consistent screen size, thin device edges, rounded corners, tiny black Dynamic Island, discreet home indicator; straight-on views, no perspective. Large enough to assess layout. One small title at top: "Juggle Dude / Splash studies". Below it a restrained subtitle "Six directions · dark + light". Clear bold numbered group titles directly above each pair; tiny "DARK" / "LIGHT" labels outside the screens. No extra annotations.
Dark screens: near-black charcoal #101313, white mark and white type, acid-lime #CDFA58 accent only.
Light screens: warm ivory #F7F8F2, charcoal mark and charcoal type, same lime accent. Every paired layout should match geometry and sizing.

01 — Pure Lift: mark alone at the optical center, confident modest scale around 32% of screen width; very generous empty space; no wordmark anywhere inside this pair.
02 — Signature: centered vertical lockup of the mark above a precise contemporary SF-like semibold "Juggle Dude" wordmark. Modest logo at 31% screen width. Balanced gap, clean flat background. This is the clearest all-round direction.
03 — Soft Halo: the same refined center lockup, with one large extremely subtle diffused lime glow behind it, elegant depth instead of a busy gradient. Dark version charcoal green atmospheric center; light version a barely visible lime haze on ivory. Keep edges clean and text sharp.
04 — Lower Signature: logo alone at optical center, and a small "Juggle Dude" wordmark near the bottom safe area, above the home indicator. Confident editorial space. No extra text.
05 — Lift Arc: centered logo plus small Juggle Dude wordmark, and one extremely fine cropped lime motion arc curving upward behind the ball, subtle and static. The arc adds lift without clutter, UI widgets, or multiple rings.
06 — Pitch Lines: centered logo plus Juggle Dude wordmark, with only the bottom third showing a very faint, oversized cropped football center-circle and field-line geometry, tonal charcoal-on-charcoal / light gray-on-ivory. Simple graphic design, not an illustrated stadium or a detailed field. No perspective grid.

Typography: crisp contemporary Apple-like sans serif. Exact in-screen text only "Juggle Dude" on concepts 02 through 06, no text inside concept 01. Keep board titles spelled exactly as given.
Style: premium minimal football training app, sleek sports identity, feasible to build with static vector shapes/gradients, excellent negative space, polished design studio contact sheet.
Constraints: six numbered groups, each contains a matching dark and light screen. Preserve the approved logo in every screen. Make the six layouts visibly distinguishable but keep one brand family. No photos, no people, no football player faces, no new logos, no fabricated features. No navigation bars, sign-in buttons, slogans, progress bars, spinners, fake percentage readouts, analytics, neon sci-fi HUDs, heavy shadows or splash animation storyboards. No app implementation.
