Create ONE polished contact sheet containing TEN NEW iPhone juggling leaderboard designs for the app "Juggle Dude". This is the next exploration in a sporty, expressive football design family. Number these new concepts 11 through 20 to make comparison easy. The user wants more fun, athletic, distinctive views, with the visual appeal of premium football culture and sports graphics. Do not produce ten ordinary tables with different headings.

Composition: exactly five columns and two rows, ten complete front-facing flat mobile app screens, equal size at approximately 1:2.1 portrait aspect ratio. No perspective, no hands, no phone hardware frames. Uniform margins and gutters on a charcoal presentation canvas. Use highest available resolution with sharp legible typography, ideally a near-square 2880 x 2880 board. Top title "Juggle Dude", subtitle "LEADERBOARDS / SPORT COLLECTION 02". Small text "11–20 · concept data". External numbered concept captions below each screen. No extra slogans or paragraphs around the board.

Overall art direction: premium football editorial design meets an appealing native mobile app. Charcoal, off-white and vivid acid lime are the core. Use fine grain, crisp sports typography, restrained chamfered corners, strong oversized rank numbers, modern player portraits and a small number of carefully placed diagonal graphic details. A little cobalt, warm orange or brushed silver may support individual concepts. Expressive yet composed, not a chaotic gaming dashboard. 6 dark screens and 4 light screens. Strong visual differences between layouts, with each screen recognizable in silhouette. A few compact translucent controls are welcome; avoid filling everything with glossy glass.

Portraits should look natural, not superhero characters. Small portraits and one or two compact winner cutouts, not huge images covering all the UI.
- Keep navigation touch targets comfortably sized and interactive content within margins. Screens should read as polished app views, not posters.
- Different list components and winner arrangements are the focus; do not just recolor the same layout.
