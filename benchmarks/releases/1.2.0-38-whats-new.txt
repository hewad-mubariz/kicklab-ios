APP REVIEW NOTES (English)

Juggle Dude counts football juggling touches with the iPhone camera and lets you edit the
replay with effects, balls, sounds and graphs. No sign-in is needed: tap "Try it first" on
the welcome screen.

App Tracking Transparency: the app uses Meta's SDK (FacebookCore) to measure Facebook and
Instagram install ads. The system tracking prompt appears once, on the home screen, after
the first finished training session, or on the third launch if none has been finished.
To see it: tap Juggling, record a few seconds, stop and close the replay; the prompt follows
on the home screen. Profile > Settings > Advertising measurement shows the choice and links
to iPhone Settings. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland (by App Store country) nothing is sent
to Meta unless tracking is allowed. Videos, touch counts and account details are never sent.

Pro (monthly or yearly auto-renewing subscription) unlocks the paid editor looks: decorative
counter styles, replacement balls, ball effects and graph styles. Capture, the touch
counter, replay, imports, history and basic video export work without a subscription.

New in this version: replay Looks, sounds and effects; a Download button that offers a
faster 1080p version or the original for 4K and slow-motion clips; and a banner that
announces newer App Store versions using Apple's public lookup.
