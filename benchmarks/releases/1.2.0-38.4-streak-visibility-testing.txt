CHECK ON BOTH IPHONE 11 AND IPHONE 17
[ ] Normal continuous juggling: no visibility notice and a plausible best streak.
[ ] Brief loss of view (about 1–2 seconds) while continuing to juggle: should not
    split for visibility alone. A separately detected catch/bounce still splits.
[ ] Leave the view for 3–4 seconds, then resume: touches on either side should
    be separate streaks, and a visibility explanation should appear.
[ ] Repeat the longer departure twice: each gap separates runs; only the longest

[ ] High kicks with your body visible: the gap between touches, or the ball going
    offscreen, must not by itself end a streak.
[ ] Keep training until the phone is warm: watch recording responsiveness and

[ ] Save, reopen History and Edit Replay: the saved best streak and visibility
    explanation should remain consistent; re-editing must not recalculate a score.
[ ] Leave the app immediately after Stop during an uncertain take: it must not
    crash or lose the recording. An unfinished check may leave an explicit
    unverified gap and separate the runs.

IMPLEMENTED POLICY
- Fresh successful detector results retain recording-relative source timestamps,
  including results too late for live counting. No wall-clock absence timeout.
- Six or more independent missing-person observations spanning at least 2.5 s,
  with no sample gap above 600 ms, separate runs.
- Cached boxes, failed inference, skipped frames and missing balls do not supply
  negative player observations. Fresh visible-person evidence resets the window.
- Unknown long intervals between contacts are checked in the saved original,
  reusing the warm model after recording. No extra live inference is scheduled.
- Rechecks sample at 2 Hz with a per-session limit of 60 frames and 6 s admission
  time. One decode/inference already in progress may finish beyond that time.
- New rechecks stop when the app is inactive, using its existing foreground/GPU
  permission gate. The original remains saved if a check cannot finish.
- If video is unavailable or the check cannot resolve continuity within the
  budget, the app labels the gap unverified and keeps the runs separate. It does
  not claim a drop or departure. Previous touches and session total remain.
- Existing hold, hand and ground-contact rules still apply. New scores use
  streak-v3; old scores and TestFlight build 38 are not rewritten.

LIMITS OF THIS TRIAL
The visibility check currently asks whether ANY person is detected. It is a
conservative empty-view check, not identity verification: a bystander remaining
in view can conceal the active player's departure. It also does not prove that
an unseen ball stayed airborne. Test the threshold in real filming conditions
before treating this as a final anti-cheat rule.

Diagnostics: Library/Caches/StreakVisibility in the app container retains small
reports for the last 20 sessions. No video pixels or uploads are added by this.
