Juggle Dude 1.0.1 (27) - App Review Notes

CHANGES IN THIS UPDATE
This update refreshes the homepage: a single "Your training" heading, a white background in Light Mode, a simpler personal-best section and a standard Session history chevron. Dark Mode remains supported. Recording, touch counting, ball detection, effects, replay/export, subscriptions and sign-in behavior are unchanged from approved build 26. The App Store screenshots and preview have also been updated.

ACCESS AND TESTING
Choose "Try it first" to use the app as a guest. No Juggle Dude account is required for recording, importing, analysis, replay/export, local history, purchasing Pro or restoring purchases.

Sample juggling clip:
https://drive.google.com/file/d/1E5P2OyqE0h4ok44QDoOuYmd7jQw-Uud-/view?usp=sharing
Save the clip to Photos, then choose Home > Import a juggling video. Keep the app open during analysis. In replay, the Download arrow saves the exported video to Photos. This lets you test without performing the exercise yourself.

Home > Juggling opens live recording. Home > Ball Distance opens shot recording and the Power Shot replay editor. Shot mode does not provide verified shot-speed or travel-distance measurements. Automatic touch estimates depend on visibility, lighting and framing.

FREE AND PRO ACCESS
Free juggling includes recording/import, analysis, local history, the basic live counter/timer and the Normal counter/timer in replay and export. Pro unlocks decorative counters, ball replacement, additional juggling effects and graphs.
Free Power Shot includes recording, the original video, basic export and Glow Trail. Other shot effects, camera tools and graphs require Pro.
Profile > Juggle Dude Pro or a locked replay option opens the paywall. Monthly and Yearly auto-renewing plans have identical benefits and no free trial. StoreKit supplies localized prices. Restore Purchases and subscription management are available in the app. Pro entitlement is separate from app sign-in.

ACCOUNTS AND ON-DEVICE ANALYSIS
Optional Sign in with Apple syncs profiles and supported results. Videos remain local, and guest history stays separate. Profile > Delete account uses Apple verification, deletes the account and its associated server results and account-local history/replays, then signs out. Guest history and Photos exports remain. Apple subscriptions are managed separately and are not cancelled by deleting the app account.
RF-DETR Seg Small runs through Apple Core ML for ball/person detection and masks; Apple Vision supplies pose. Video/frame analysis stays on-device. Model licence and attribution notices are bundled with the app.
