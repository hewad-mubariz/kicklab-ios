IMG8874, low and raw-juggling have zero fallback calls yet their paired counts vary. That establishes a continuing comparator/timing variability problem, not a measured fallback-caused regression.

DECISION:
Do not promote the legacy-on-empty fallback yet. It demonstrably restores measured poses (35/35 selected matches in the exercised IMG8606 run, 18/20 in the dribbling control) and the negative controls remain zero, but does not establish repeatable legal-touch recovery. The first IMG8606 pair loses two legal contacts; the reversed pair gains two while hardly exercising fallback. Other clips vary despite zero fallback calls. Keep the explicit flag off and restore the previous field app.

The concrete advance is the exact-input reproduction: this dropout occurs before player selection and counting, with identical image bytes and no in-call mutation. Recreating the request, changing image order, removing the bridge, PNG input and explicit Neural Engine assignment did not reliably solve it. Cropping improves isolated detections but still has misses. The internal framework/context cause remains unresolved.

Next bounded work should test a generic selected-player pose strategy on these frozen images with repeatable raw observations and correct image-to-player coordinates, then run the unchanged contact rules and labelled corpus. Keep the independent candidate-timing/long-pose-call issue separate. Do not weaken hand or ground vetoes to hide a missing-pose problem.

LIMITS: Development contact labels, small selected corpus, iPhone17 only. No iPhone11 qualification or 15-minute camera/thermal claim. A higher total does not prove preservation of every legal contact. Existing ground false positives must be reported separately from new gains.
