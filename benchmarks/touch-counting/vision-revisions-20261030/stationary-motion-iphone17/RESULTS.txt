Status: EXPERIMENTAL / NOT PROMOTED. Main app and TestFlight defaults unchanged.
Empty-pose, crop, fallback and scheduling experiments remain parked/off.

WHY THIS WAS TESTED
The original reviewed revision-2 cohort has 80 missed labelled legal contacts. Of these, 34 have no nearby candidate and 16 are labelled amplitudeTooSmall. Those diagnostic labels are leads, not independently verified root causes. Empty-pose failures are not the main target of this experiment.

CONFIRMED FINDING
The existing V2 stationary gate fixes permanent first-frame anchors by removing an anchor after two unoccupied detector replies. But a ball already making very low kicks can remain within half a ball diameter of its first position. In low.mov the detector correctly sees it moving, while the gate suppresses its first 1.3 seconds and continues suppressing returns until 2.267 seconds. The video was inspected visually; this is the actual juggled ball.

ISOLATED CHANGE
Debug-only --stationary-gate-motion-v3 releases an anchor after four consecutive reliable observations show three movements inside the original exclusion radius. It requires finite geometry, confidence >=0.8, stable box dimensions, 25–150 ms between observations, movement >=0.15 diameter on each step and >=0.25 diameter total spatial spread. Missing or ambiguous detections reset the evidence. A single jump or accumulated small jitter cannot release the spot. The ordinary V2 disappearance path is preserved.
This changes tracking eligibility only. It does not count a touch, relax ground/hand/holding rules, change pose revision, or call a new model. Recovered contacts still incur their normal pose/contact work.

VALIDATION SCOPE
Four initial diagnostic captures on build 2026103007, followed by 24 paired real-time original-source replays on build 2026103008: ten distinct clips, with repeated low and IMG8606 pairs. Second repeats reverse arm order. All paired runs completed at nominal/fair temperature with exact counter-trace replay, no quality failure and zero queue overflow. This is iPhone 17 only; not a fresh full-corpus or iPhone 11 sign-off.

WHAT THE COUNTS MEAN
Both low.mov candidate runs recover the same five labelled opening kicks: 0.70, 0.98, 1.33, 1.66 and 1.94 seconds. Tracking begins at 0.30 instead of 1.30 seconds; suppressed detector replies fall from 23 to 3. Each candidate run counts 163/195 legal contacts (83.6%) versus 159/195 (81.5%) and 158/195 (81.0%) in the paired controls. The five opening gains are repeatable; these are not claims of five net extra touches in every run.
Later differences remain. Both low pairs lose 23.11, 28.06 and 37.43 seconds relative to their paired controls. The first pair also loses 4.67 seconds; the second baseline also misses that event. Other later gains/losses are listed in summary.json. These differences cannot be dismissed because the total improves.
On the first 4.67-second pair, the current run uses RF at 4.567/4.667 s; the candidate uses RF at 4.600/4.700 s. The measured boxes and smoothed turn differ. The candidate never produces the contact: its smoothed rise remains below the ordinary rise threshold and does not meet the stronger impulse guard for the early small-rise probe. No pose-empty result is needed to explain this miss. The saved motion/box evidence is low-4670-input-timing.json.
On identical recorded detector inputs, the new gate makes no eligibility changes to the other nine tested clips. Their fresh-run count differences, including IMG8606 and eighteen, cannot be credited as recoveries caused by this gate rule. IMG8606 retains its existing labelled false count at about 20.176 seconds in every paired run.
All five negative controls (held ball and four dribbling clips) remain zero. No new labelled false or unmatched counts were observed in these paired runs. This does not prove safety on untested footage.

PERFORMANCE
The low-clip frame pipeline p95 stays about 223–225 ms in both arms. Contact-notification p95 is 605/672 ms in the controls and 607/641 ms in the candidates. Peak counter queue depth is 13/16 versus 17/19 (capacity 32), with zero overflow. Pose calls rise from 1661/1666 to 1735/1737 as the recovered start is processed. No claim of zero additional total workload, or of iPhone 11 performance, is made.

DECISION / NEXT TARGET
Keep this branch-only flag off in ordinary recordings. The startup fix is real and useful, but the combined live pipeline has not met the user requirement to recover contacts without losing others. Next, make measured short-contact confirmation less sensitive to detector sampling around the impact, using positive foot evidence and unchanged hand/ground vetoes; use the saved paired low clips as regression inputs. Do not globally lower rejection thresholds or add per-clip timestamps. Re-run the reviewed corpus and iPhone 11 before promotion.
